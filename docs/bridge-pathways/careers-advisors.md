### How Careers advising works
Careers is part of Pathways, alongside Finance. Finance stays as Finance, keeps its own documents, and is not changed by this list.
Careers runs one to one, built around each student athlete's own interest, and serves all ages, including high schoolers.
Entry is through the Careers application, which is separate from the Finance application. On it, the athlete names the area he is interested in.
Before one to one work starts, the athlete has a conversation with his potential advisor. It is an evaluation conversation, not an interview. It checks whether the interest is the right fit, whether it is the right profession for him, and whether he has the wrong idea about what the work actually is.
The conversation is between the advisor and the athlete only.
Bridge connects and prepares. Bridge does not guarantee an outcome. An employer hires and decides.
