THE BRIDGE FOUNDATION FOR STUDENT ATHLETES

## Bridge Pathways
Two sides. One program. Every part free.

### Every Kid Leaves With a Plan
Most of our athletes will not play their sport for a living. That is not a failure, it is just math. Bridge Pathways exists so every kid who comes through the Bridge Foundation leaves with a real plan for money and for work, whether that plan runs through college, a trade, or straight into a career.
The program has two sides. Finance teaches real life money skills. Careers connects each athlete, one to one, with an advisor in the field he is interested in, and Trades sits inside Careers as one of its branches. Each side has its own documents and its own application, under one Pathways umbrella. A student athlete can be in one side, or in both.
Bridge connects and prepares. Bridge never guarantees a job, a placement, or an outcome. Where work is involved, the employer hires and decides, on the employer's terms.

#### Finance
Money skills for student athletes, taught by people who work with money every day. Finance runs in eight week phases, is for Bridge athletes only, and takes roughly 10 to 15 athletes in a round. Full details are in the Bridge Pathways: Finance program document.

#### Careers
One to one career advising built around what each athlete is actually interested in, for high school athletes, college athletes, and alumni. It starts with an application and a required conversation with his potential advisor, and it includes Trades. Full details are in the Bridge Pathways: Careers program document.

### Side One. Finance: Money Skills for Student Athletes
Finance is a simple, practical program that teaches real life money skills. Each phase is eight weekly sessions, 45 minutes each, on video, Wednesdays at 8:00 PM. Sessions are recorded so athletes can watch them again. There are no lectures, no tests, and no grades. Light work is assigned between sessions and is not graded. Finance is for Bridge athletes only, with roughly 10 to 15 athletes in a round.

#### The Eight Weeks
Week 1. Financial Aid, with Kevin Valdovinos
Week 2. NIL and Scholarships, with Kevin Valdovinos
Week 3. Managing Money in College, with Wei Chang
Week 4. Loans, with Byron Valverde
Week 5. How to Save, with Sameer
Week 6. How to Budget, with Byron Valverde
Week 7. Paychecks and Taxes, with Sameer
Week 8. Credit, with Wei Chang
Weeks 1 through 4 are required for anyone not in college. Weeks 5 through 8 are optional but strongly suggested.
Athletes are never asked to talk about their family money, and nobody contacts an athlete's school about the program. Finance teaches money skills. It is guidance, not advice on any one athlete's personal finances.
Entry is simple. He fills out the Finance application and he is in. Finance has no entry conversation and no added steps.

### Side Two. Careers: One to One, Built Around His Interest
Careers serves all ages, high school athletes included, along with college athletes and alumni. Dave Fisher runs Careers for now. It does not run as a class and it does not run as a group. Each athlete works one to one with an advisor in the area he is interested in, and Bridge connects him with people who actually do that work.

#### The Categories
Trades. Plumbing, electrical, auto body, construction, and the other skilled trades. RB Plumbing is the founding partner.
Sports and Fitness. Coaching, training, facility work, and the business around sport.
Business. Marketing, finance, sales, operations, and office work.
Entrepreneurship. Building your own thing, and what that really takes.
Media. Content, production, and the work behind the camera and the microphone.
Public Service. Government, community, and public facing roles.
There is no Tech category. Bridge does not have a real tech person behind it yet, and the program does not promise what it cannot deliver. A category is only offered with real people behind it.

#### Trades, Inside Careers
College is one path. Trades is another. Inside Careers, Bridge connects athletes and alumni with licensed trade professionals for apprenticeships and job placement. College graduates get placement. High school athletes get apprenticeships, shaped to what is legal and right at their age. Anyone placed works for the trade partner, under that partner's rules. Full details are in the Trades document, a branch of Careers.

### How Careers Works: Apply, Talk, Then Start
He applies. Careers has its own application, separate from the Finance application. On it, he names the category he is interested in, Trades included.
He has the conversation. Before anything else happens, he sits down with his potential advisor in that area. Just the two of them.
The one to one work begins. Advising, honest guidance on the real path into the field, and connections through the advisor network.

#### The Conversation Is Required, and It Is Not an Interview
The conversation is an evaluation of the interest itself. Is this the right fit for him? Is it the right profession? Does he have the wrong idea about what the work actually is? Nobody starts the one to one work until that conversation has happened. It protects the athlete from chasing the wrong thing, and it protects the advisor from being handed someone who has not been checked against the reality of the field.
These advisors already talk with these athletes all the time. The conversation is an ordinary advisor conversation, held the way those conversations have always been held.

#### The Advisors
Careers starts with the Bridge board: Dave, Byron, Way, Kev, Mike, and DJ. The full, current list of advisors lives in the Bridge Pathways: Careers Advisors list, which Dave updates as the network grows. Trades stands on its partners, starting with RB Plumbing.

### How to Apply: Separate Applications, One Umbrella
Fill out the application for the side you want. Finance and Careers each have their own application.
You may apply to both sides. Being in Finance does not close Careers, and being in Careers does not close Finance.
Bridge reviews the application and follows up. For Careers, the next step after the application is the advisor conversation.

### Who It Is For: Current Athletes and Alumni
Finance is for Bridge athletes only, any gender. Careers serves high school athletes, college athletes, and alumni, with age appropriate placement for each. Trades serves both high school athletes and college graduates.

### Cost: Free, All of It, With Real Support Behind It
Every side of Bridge Pathways is free to participants. Bridge also supports Careers participants financially. Where a real cost on the path is more than a participant can afford, Bridge helps cover it.

### Safeguarding: Safety Is Not Optional
Parental consent is required for all participants under 18.
Two Bridge adults are on every Finance virtual session, no exceptions.
Finance sessions are recorded so athletes can watch them again. Group sessions only, with no one to one breakouts.
Trades placements for minors are limited to legal, age appropriate work, subject to legal review before any minor is placed.
Careers conversations are advisor conversations between advisors and athletes who already know each other.

### About the Foundation
The Bridge Foundation for Student Athletes is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit in Stamford, Connecticut. Founded in 2023, the Foundation expands college access and opportunity for underserved student athletes through recruiting guidance, academic support, athletic development, and direct financial assistance. Bridge Pathways is its career readiness program.
