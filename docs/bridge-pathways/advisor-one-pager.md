
## Bridge Pathways Careers
Advisor Overview | The Bridge Foundation for Student Athletes
Pathways helps Bridge student athletes build a life after sport. It has two sides. Finance teaches athletes how money works, and it stays exactly as it is. Careers is the new side. It helps an athlete turn a real interest into a real direction for work. Trades sits under Careers as one of its categories.

### Careers runs one to one, around each athlete
Careers serves athletes of all ages, high schoolers included. There is no group class and no set season. Each athlete works one to one with an advisor in the area he is interested in. Dave Fisher runs Careers for now, and the first advisors come from the Bridge board.

### The six categories
Category | What it covers
Trades | Skilled trades, including the partners already working with Bridge.
Sports and Fitness | Coaching, training, and work in sports and fitness.
Business | Business, office, and operations work.
Entrepreneurship | Starting and running a business of your own.
Media | Media and content work.
Public Service | Public service and community roles.
There is no Tech category. A category is only offered when a real advisor stands behind it, and Bridge would rather leave a category out than promise one it cannot deliver.

### What an advisor does
Start with the evaluation conversation, one to one with the athlete who has applied and named your area. It is a conversation, not an interview. Test the interest itself. Is this the right fit for him? Is it the right profession for him? Does he have the wrong idea about what the work actually is? A clear no here helps him as much as a yes.
Advise one to one. Work with him one to one from there. Share what the work is really like, help him get ready, and help him take the next honest step in that field.
Be straight. Tell him the truth, including when a path is not right for him. That honesty is the value of the program.

### What Bridge handles
Careers is free for every athlete. There is no fee to take part.
Where a real cost on the path is more than an athlete can afford, Bridge helps cover it.
Bridge makes the connection and helps the athlete prepare. Bridge does not run your workplace and does not direct your work.
Plain promises. Bridge connects and prepares. Bridge never guarantees a job, a placement, or an outcome. An employer hires and decides, on the employer's terms. Please keep that same plain language in every conversation about Careers.
