THE BRIDGE FOUNDATION FOR STUDENT ATHLETES

## Bridge Pathways
Money skills for college. A real start on the work you want to do.
One program, two sides. Do Finance, do Careers, or do both. Everything is free.

### Finance
Learn what money actually does, before college asks you to decide.
Eight weekly sessions. 45 minutes each, on video, and recorded so you can rewatch or catch up.
Weeks 1 to 4 are required if you are not in college yet: Financial Aid, NIL and Scholarships, Managing Money in College, and Loans.
Weeks 5 to 8 are optional but strongly suggested: How to Save, How to Budget, Paychecks and Taxes, and Credit.
No tests, no grades. Light work to make it stick, taught through real conversation and real examples.
Private by rule. You are never asked about your family's money, and nobody contacts your school. Two Bridge adults are on every call. Under 18, a parent or guardian signs off first.

### Careers
One to one, built around what you are actually interested in.
Open to all ages, high school included. Pick your area:
Trades. Hands on work and apprenticeships, including plumbing with RB Plumbing.
Sports and Fitness. Coaching, training, and facility work.
Business. The business side of sports and beyond, from marketing to operations.
Entrepreneurship. Starting and running something of your own.
Media. Content, coverage, and production.
Public Service. Work that serves your community.

### How Careers Starts
Apply. Fill out the Careers application and name the area you are interested in. Careers has its own application, separate from Finance, and you can be in both sides of Pathways at once.
Talk it through. Before anything else, you sit down with your potential advisor for an evaluation conversation. It is not an interview. You check together whether it is the right fit, whether it is the right profession for you, and whether you might have the wrong idea about what the work really is.
Start one to one. If it is the right fit, the one to one work starts. Your advisor helps you get ready, makes real connections, and stays in your corner while you take the next steps.
Free, and we help with the costs that get in the way. There is no cost to apply and no cost to take part. Where a real cost on your path is more than you can afford, Bridge helps cover it.
Honest about what Bridge does. Bridge connects you and helps you get ready. Bridge does not guarantee a job, and applying does not get anyone hired. The employer hires and decides, and anyone placed works for that employer, under that employer's rules.
Who can join: Finance is for current Bridge athletes. Careers is open to Bridge athletes and alumni at every age, including while you are still in high school.
Your advisors: Careers advising starts with the Bridge board: Dave, Byron, Wei, Kev, Mike, and DJ. These are advisors who already know and talk with Bridge athletes every day.
