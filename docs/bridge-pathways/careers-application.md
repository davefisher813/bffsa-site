### 5. What Happens Next
You apply. Fill out this form, sign it, and return it to the Bridge Foundation.
You talk with your potential advisor. This conversation is required, and it comes before anything else. It is not an interview. It is an evaluation of the interest itself: is this the right fit for you, is it the right profession, and do you have the wrong idea about what the work actually is? The conversation is between you and the advisor.
The one to one work starts. If the fit is right, you and your advisor work one to one from there, built around your interest and your next steps.
Careers is free. Where a real cost on your path is more than you can afford, Bridge helps cover it. Tell us about it and we will work it out with you.
